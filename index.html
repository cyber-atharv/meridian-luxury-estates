About Meridian

Building lives
on the coast
since 1998.

For over two decades, Meridian Development Group has designed and delivered homes that don't merely occupy the coastline — they belong to it. We build where others hesitate, because we understand the forces at play.

Founded in Malibu, California, our practice spans the full arc — from raw land acquisition through bespoke design, structural build, and long-term stewardship.

How We Build

Six stages.
One standard.

01Site Selection

Land

We identify parcels with irreplaceable natural attributes — the exact angle of morning light, the way a headland holds the wind.

02Engineering

Foundation

Below grade, everything is overbuilt. Concrete, piling, and structure designed to last centuries on demanding coastline.

03Build

Structure

Our master-build team holds tolerances that luxury joinery demands. We bring the architect's vision off the page with obsessive precision.

04Envelope

Facade

Each facade is tuned to its environment — thermal mass, weather resistance, and the quality of light that enters every room.

05Finish

Interiors

We collaborate with the world's finest interior studios. Every surface is chosen for its ability to age beautifully.

06Delivery

Handover

Our relationship doesn't end at settlement. We stand behind every Meridian home — for as long as it stands.
